🗺️ Story Architect build your own choose-your-own-adventure

Hi, Architect! 👋

You're about to build a book where the reader picks what happens next. That makes you the architect — you design every path, door, and dead end.

Engineer secret: a choose-your-own-adventure is really a flowchart. Programmers call this a decision tree or a directed graph. Every scene is a node, every choice is an arrow pointing to the next node. You're building the same kind of map a video game uses to decide what happens when you press a button. Pretty cool job title for a 12-year-old. 😎

Work through the stops below in order. Each one teaches a piece, then sends you to do it. Tap a stop to open it. ✅ marks the ones you finish.

My Adventure

How scenes work: Scene #1 is always your beginning (gold). Write what happens, then add choices. Each choice is a door — point it to another scene by its number. A scene with no doors out is an ending. 🏁

🎤 Voice typing: tap Talk on any scene and just speak — your words land in the box. Tap ⏹ Stop when done and it auto-tidies capitals and a final period. Say "new line" for a new line. (This one feature needs an internet connection.)

💡 Reading the map: follow an arrow from a box to see where that choice sends the reader. Gold arrows are loops — they send readers back to a scene they already passed. That's how you let someone "go back and try the other door."
